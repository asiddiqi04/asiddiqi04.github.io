About

Curious by default, builder by habit.

Hi, I'm Adil, a Software Engineering co-op student at the University of Guelph with a passion for using technology to solve real-world problems. From a young age, I've always been fascinated by how things work, and I've always had a passion for solving problems and thinking of new solutions to problems I've had. This led me to pursue a career in software engineering, where I can apply my curiosity and love for innovation.

Through my studies and co-op placements, I've gained hands-on experience in software development, automation, and data systems. At Lafarge Canada I worked as a Business Intelligence Analyst, blending technical skills with business insight to streamline operations and drive data-driven decisions. At TextNow I spent two consecutive terms on the AI & Internal Tools team, where I learned what it takes to build systems an organization depends on: reliability, security, and documentation.

When I'm not coding or working on projects, I enjoy spending time playing all types of sports. Growing up I found myself on the basketball court and soccer field most of the time, but recently I've found a passion for going to the gym and boxing. These sports help me stay fit and sharpen my focus and discipline. Both have been essential to my academic and professional success.

In addition to staying active, I have a deep love for reading. Whether it's non-fiction about the natural world or novels where I can dive into different worlds, reading fuels my creative thinking and helps me relax. It's a habit that has benefited me greatly.

I'm always looking for new challenges that let me combine technical skill and creativity to make an impact. Feel free to explore my projects and work term reports, and don't hesitate to connect if you'd like to collaborate or discuss opportunities.
