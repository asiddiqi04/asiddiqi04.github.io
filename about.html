A bit about me

Hi, I'm Adil, a Software Engineering co-op student at the University of Guelph. I enjoy building technology that solves practical problems, particularly across software development, automation, AI, and data systems.

Through my studies and co-op experiences, I've had the opportunity to contribute on software used in production. At Lafarge Canada, I worked as a Business Intelligence Analyst, using automation and data to streamline operations and support better decision-making. At TextNow, I spent two consecutive terms on the AI & Internal Tools team, where I gained experience building systems with an emphasis on reliability, security, and maintainability.

These experiences have shaped how I approach software engineering: understand the problem, build something useful, and make sure it works well beyond the initial implementation.

I'm always interested in opportunities to tackle challenging problems, learn new technologies, and build software that has a meaningful impact. Feel free to explore my projects and work term reports to see more of what I've worked on.
